Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

TeslaNav has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.

Location

TeslaNav reads your iPhone's location to show it on the navigation page in your car. It is sent only over your iPhone's Personal Hotspot to the browser that opened the page — a direct, local connection between your phone and your car. It never goes to the developer.

Screen mirroring

When you start a broadcast, images of your screen are passed from TeslaNav's broadcast extension to the app on the same iPhone and streamed to the car's browser over the hotspot. They are not recorded or sent anywhere else. Only the latest frame is kept, and it is deleted when the broadcast stops.

Maps, search and routes

The page in your car loads map images and asks for search results and routes directly from public OpenStreetMap-based services:

  • Map images: OpenStreetMap tile servers (privacy policy) or, if you choose it, Esri (privacy policy).
  • Place search: Nominatim, run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
  • Routes: the public OSRM routing service (project-osrm.org).

These services receive what they need to answer: the map area being viewed, the text you search for, and the start and end points of a route, together with your connection's IP address. They are operated independently of TeslaNav under their own policies.

Tips

“Buy me a coffee” is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal information.

Stored on your devices

The car page remembers your recent destinations, map position and settings in the car browser's local storage. You can clear them by clearing that browser's data.

Children

TeslaNav collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
