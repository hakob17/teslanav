Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026 (screen mirroring through the relay)

HotspotNav has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer does not collect or store any of your data.

Location

The navigation page in your car uses the car browser's own location, with your permission, to show where you are and guide you. It stays in the browser and is never sent to the developer. The iPhone app does not use your location.

Screen mirroring

When you start a broadcast and the car page is paired with your iPhone's code, your iPhone screen is encoded as video and sent to the car through HotspotNav's relay, which runs on Cloudflare. The relay only forwards the stream in real time between your phone and your car; it does not record or store it. Nothing is sent while no car is watching. Anyone who knows your 8-character pairing code could watch while you broadcast, so keep it private; you can create a new code in the app at any time.

Maps, search and routes

The page in your car is served by GitHub Pages and loads map images, search results and routes directly from public OpenStreetMap-based services:

  • Map images: OpenStreetMap tile servers (privacy policy) or, if you choose it, Esri (privacy policy).
  • Place search: Nominatim, run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
  • Routes: the public OSRM routing service (project-osrm.org).

These services receive what they need to answer: the map area being viewed, the text you search for, and the start and end points of a route, together with your connection's IP address. They are operated independently of HotspotNav under their own policies.

Tips

“Buy me a coffee” is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details or personal information.

Stored on your devices

The car page remembers your recent destinations, map position and settings in the car browser's local storage. You can clear them by clearing that browser's data.

Children

HotspotNav collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
