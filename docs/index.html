Navigation on your car's big screen

TeslaNav turns your iPhone into a small navigation server. Turn on Personal Hotspot, open one bookmark in the car's browser, and get a live map with search, turn-by-turn directions, voice prompts and arrival time — driven by your iPhone's GPS. No cloud, no account.

Getting started

  1. On the iPhone, turn on Personal Hotspot, and join it from the car's Wi-Fi settings.
  2. Open TeslaNav once and allow location access.
  3. In the car's browser, open http://172.20.10.1:8080 and bookmark it.
  4. Search a destination on the car screen and drive. You can switch apps or lock the phone.

Mirror mode

Want live traffic from your favourite navigation app? Tap Start Broadcast in TeslaNav, then Phone screen on the car page. Your whole iPhone screen appears in the car.

Troubleshooting

  • The page doesn't load: check the car is joined to your iPhone's hotspot and TeslaNav has been opened since the phone restarted.
  • “Lost connection to iPhone”: open TeslaNav on the phone again; iOS may have closed it.
  • No search results or map: the car uses your iPhone's mobile data through the hotspot — check you have signal.
