Navigation on your car's big screen

Open one page in the car's browser and get a live night map with search, turn-by-turn directions, voice prompts and arrival time, using the car's own GPS. Want live traffic from your usual navigation app? The TeslaNav iPhone app mirrors your whole screen to the car.

Open the car page →  (hakob17.github.io/teslanav/car)

Getting started

  1. In the car's browser, open hakob17.github.io/teslanav/car and bookmark it with ☆.
  2. Allow location when asked, search a destination and drive.

Mirror mode

  1. Open TeslaNav on the iPhone and tap Start Broadcast.
  2. On the car page tap Phone screen and enter the 8-character code shown in the app (only the first time).
  3. Switch to your navigation app on the phone. The car shows your screen within a second or two.

Mirroring uses mobile data while the car is watching (roughly 0.5 GB per hour).

Troubleshooting

  • No location on the map: allow location for the page in the car's browser.
  • “Waiting for the iPhone…”: check the broadcast is running and the code in the car matches the app (menu ⋯ → Phone code).
  • No map or search: the car needs internet — its own connection or your iPhone's hotspot.
